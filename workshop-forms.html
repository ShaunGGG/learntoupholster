Workshop forms

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Four forms that carry a job through the workshop: the enquiry, the condition report, the job sheet, and the note that gets signed at handover. Free, no download and no account — fill them in on screen and print, or print them blank and fill them in at the bench.

Put your details on them

Saved in this browser only, and never sent anywhere.

  • Customer enquiry

    Taken on the first phone call or email, so nothing is forgotten before you quote.

  • Furniture condition report

    Completed before you touch the piece. The form that prevents arguments about damage.

  • Job sheet

    Travels with the furniture: scope, materials, fire certificates, hours and progress.

  • Collection & delivery note

    Signed at handover. Two copies, one each.

The quote and invoice are covered separately by the free quote and invoice spreadsheet, which does the arithmetic. These four are the workshop half — the paper that stays with you.

Written for use anywhere. Where a form mentions fire certificates or labels, check what applies in your own country — the fire regulations checker covers the UK position.
