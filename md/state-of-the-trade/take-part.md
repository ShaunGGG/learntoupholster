# Take part in the survey

*State of the Upholstery Trade*

> Add your workshop to the State of the Upholstery Trade survey. Anonymous, thirteen questions, about three minutes.

Canonical: https://www.learntoupholster.com/state-of-the-trade/take-part

Thirteen questions about how your workshop actually runs. About three minutes.

Anonymous. No name, no email address, nothing that identifies you or your business. Answer what you are comfortable with — only the first five are required, and a partial answer is still useful.

Results are published at [State of the Trade](https://www.learntoupholster.com/state-of-the-trade/) and are free for anyone to cite.
