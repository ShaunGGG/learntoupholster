Why we document every job
Textbooks show the ideal. A working bench shows the truth: the split rail nobody mentioned, the previous upholsterer’s shortcut, the frame that turned out to be worth saving after all. These pages record real work as it happened — the hours, the materials, the problems and the finish — from an AMUSF-accredited workshop in Hebden Bridge.

The full method behind every job is in [*The Working Upholsterer’s Bible*](https://www.learntoupholster.com/buy-the-book).
