## The gallery

The projects above are documented stage by stage. These are the finished pieces — forty-three of them, reupholstered by hand at Greenwood Upholstery in West Yorkshire. Victorian show-frames and Queen Anne wing chairs, mid-century settees, chaises, footstools and stools. Tap any photograph to see it larger.
