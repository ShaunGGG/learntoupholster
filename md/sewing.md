# Industrial sewing for upholsterers

*Free resource*

> Industrial sewing for upholsterers: thread, needles, machines and troubleshooting, explained from an upholstery bench rather than a sail loft.

Canonical: https://www.learntoupholster.com/sewing

Industrial sewing explained for upholsterers. Most of what is written about these machines is aimed at sailmakers, leatherworkers or garment factories — this is the same ground from an upholstery bench.

## Thread

Bonded nylon or polyester, Tex sizes and the three numbering systems, which thread for which job, and the twist question.

## Needles

Needle size by thread size, needle systems, round and leather points, and why a blunt needle causes skipped stitches.

## Thread & needle selector

Answer three questions and get the thread, size, needle and stitch length for the job.

## Machines

Walking foot, compound and needle feed, cylinder arm, flat bed — explained for upholsterers rather than sailmakers.

## Troubleshooting

Fraying, skipped stitches, uneven tension, puckering, thread breaks.

## Setup & parts

Presser feet, servo against clutch motors, speed reducers, tension and timing.

**Where to start.** If something is going wrong at the machine, it is thread or needle far more often than tension. The two guides above answer most of it, and the pairing between them answers most of the rest.
